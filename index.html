About

From mathematical foundations to applied AI.

My academic work developed from nonsmooth analysis on Riemannian manifolds to numerical algorithms for nonsmooth, nonconvex, and low-rank optimization. Following international postdoctoral research at ETH Zürich and the University of Bonn, I moved into the automotive industry to translate mathematical ideas into practical engineering solutions.

Today, I lead interdisciplinary AI projects at IAV GmbH. My work combines physical simulation and generative AI.
